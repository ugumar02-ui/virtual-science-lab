## Practical-by-practical audit

### Physics

- **Vernier Callipers — Diameter and Cross-Section Area** — Class IX  
  Physics IX · Topic 1 Measurement · activity 2  
  > To calculate the area of the cross-section of a solid cylinder by measuring the diameter with Vernier callipers.

- **Screw Gauge — Thickness of a Wire** — Class IX  
  Physics IX · Topic 1 Measurement · activity 3  
  > To measure the thickness of a metal strip or the diameter of a wire using a screw gauge.

- **Principle of Moments** — Class IX  
  Physics IX · Topic 4 Turning Effect of Forces · activity 7  
  > To find the weight of an unknown object using the principle of moments.

- **Hooke's Law — Load and Extension of a Spring** — Class IX  
  Physics IX · Topic 5 Deformation of Solids · activity 9  
  > To study the relationship between load and extension of a helical spring with the help of a graph.

- **Simple Pendulum — Determination of g** — Class X  
  Physics X · Topic 11 Wave Motion · activity 2  
  > To study the effect of the change in the length of a simple pendulum on its time period and hence calculate the value of acceleration due to gravity 'g'.

- **Resonance Tube — Speed of Sound in Air** — Class X  
  Physics X · Topic 12 Sound · activity 3  
  > To study the reflection of sound by determining the length of the air column at first loud sound using tuning forks of different frequencies and to determine the corresponding wavelength of sound.

- **Ohm's Law — Resistance of a Wire** — Class X  
  Physics X · Topic 15 Electric Current · activity 7  
  > To verify Ohm's law using a wire as a conductor.

- **Resistance in Series and Parallel** — Class X  
  Physics X · Topic 16 D.C. Circuits · activities 9–10  
  > To study resistance in a series circuit and in a parallel circuit.

- **Spherometer — Radius of Curvature** — Class XI  
  Physics XI · Topic 1 Physical Quantities and Measurement · activity 3  
  > Determine the radius of curvature of any spherical surface by using a spherometer.

- **Specific Heat Capacity by the Method of Mixtures** — Class XI  
  Physics XI · Topic 10 Thermodynamics · activity 11  
  > Determine the specific heat capacity of solid using method of mixture (Regnault's Method).

- **Tungsten Filament Lamp — Non-Ohmic Conduction** — Class XII  
  Physics XII · Topic 13 Electric Current · activity 3  
  > Investigate the relationship between current passing through a tungsten filament lamp and the potential applied across it.

- **RC Circuit — Time Constant** — Class XII  
  Physics XII · Topic 12 Electrostatics · activity 2  
  > Determine time constant by charging and discharging a capacitor through a resistor.

## What is not simulated yet

The remaining syllabus activities are mostly observational: prepared slides,
specimens, dissections, models and drawing exercises (for example the frog
specimen, plant and animal tissues, the goat heart and lung, and the kidney,
brain, eye and ear models). These need microscope images and 3D models, which
is the next phase of the project.

Two built practicals use a measurable method that differs from the syllabus
wording; both are flagged above and on the practical page:

1. **Osmosis (IX)** uses potato tissue instead of cellophane and a thistle funnel,
   because it gives a measurable isotonic point.
2. **Microscope (XI)** assesses magnification and field of view rather than
   focusing technique; a focusing simulation is planned.
